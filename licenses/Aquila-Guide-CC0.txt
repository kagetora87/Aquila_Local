Aquila Guide placeholder VRM 1.0

Original procedural geometry, materials, skeleton and facial morphs created for Aquila Local.
No third-party avatar model, motion capture or animation asset is included.
The model and its generator make_robot.py are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This is a presentation/test asset. The body is rigidly attached to hips and the head
to the head bone. It supports breathing, head motion, blink, aa and happy expressions;
it is not an authored full-body animation rig. Godot/VRM importer code has separate licenses.
